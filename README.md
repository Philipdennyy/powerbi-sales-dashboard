# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, and customer behavior using a retail dataset.

The dashboard enables interactive exploration of key business metrics and provides insights into trends, product performance, and customer contributions.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

* Designed using a **Star Schema Data Model** (Fact & Dimension tables)
* Created **DAX measures** for key KPIs:

  * Total Sales
  * Total Profit
  * Total Orders
  * Profit Margin
  * Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
* Implemented **Top N Analysis** to identify top-performing products and customers
* Added **interactive slicers** for filtering by category, segment, and date
* Built **drill-through functionality** for detailed product-level insights

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

### 🔹 Executive Summary

* KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Orders, Margin, YoY Growth)
* Sales Trend (time-based analysis)
* Category-wise performance
* Interactive filters (slicers)

---

### 🔹 Product & Customer Analysis

* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Products by Profit
* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Segment-wise distribution

---

### 🔹 Product Details (Drill-through)

* Detailed view of selected product
* Sales and Profit breakdown
* Dynamic filtering based on selection
